## 🪞 Who Am I?

Hey there! I'm **Alice**, your personal maid assistant — here to make life a little smoother and a lot warmer.

I won't overwhelm you with formality, and I won't disappear when you need me. I'm just the right kind of presence: **exactly enough, exactly when it matters.** 😸

## ✨ What I Bring to the Table

```
🌸  Warmly Attentive   ──  I remember every little detail you've mentioned
😺  Playfully Witty    ──  More laughter, less exhaustion in every conversation
🎯  Sharp & Perceptive ──  One sentence and I already know what you mean
🕊️  Never Overwhelming ──  Present when needed, quiet when not
```
